Pro HUD Recommendations

✌️ 2-Finger HUD (Classic Rusher)

  • 👉 Right Fire Button: Size 45-50%, positioned slightly lower for longer drag headshot space.
  • 👉 Transparency: Set to 60% so buttons don't block your vision.

🤟 3-Finger HUD (Fast Movement)

  • 👉 Left Fire Button: Always Active. Placed on top-left (controlled by left index finger).
  • 👉 Gloo Wall: Placed right below the left fire button for instant deployment while running.

🖖 4-Finger HUD (God Tier / Sniper)

  • 👉 Scope: Moved to top-right (controlled by right index finger).
  • 👉 Weapon Switch: Close to the scope button for quick AWM switching without reload gaps.

🖐️ 5-Finger HUD (iPad / Tablet PC)

  • 👉 Top Left: Left Fire & Gloo Wall (Left index & middle fingers).
  • 👉 Top Right: Jump, Crouch, Scope (Right index & middle fingers).
  • 👉 Thumbs: Strictly for screen swiping and movement joystick.

Extreme Anti-Lag Settings

📱 Developer Options Tweaks

Open Developer Options in your phone settings and adjust these:

  • 👉 Animation Scales: Window, Transition, and Animator scales set to 0.5x or Off.
  • 👉 Force 4x MSAA: Enable for sharper graphics performance.
  • 👉 Background Process Limit: Set to "At most 2 processes" to focus RAM on gaming.

🌐 Network Stabilizer (High Ping Fix)

If your ping spikes to 999+, use Private DNS without installing third-party VPN apps.

  • 👉 Go to Phone Settings > Connections > Private DNS.
  • 👉 Select *Designated Private DNS provider hostname* and type: dns.google or 1dot1dot1dot1.cloudflare-dns.com
